Measure Model Performance

For price prediction, use regression metrics computed from your trained model.

—

Metrics are left blank until a real model is trained and evaluated. Accuracy, precision, recall and F1 are generally classification metrics, not the primary metrics for continuous price prediction.

05 · ETHICAL REVIEW

Responsible Data Science

Document limitations and make the results transparent to users.

01

Privacy

Avoid collecting personal information that is not needed for price estimation.

02

Representation

Check whether brands, price ranges and device types are adequately represented.

03

Transparency

Show data sources, collection dates, assumptions and model limitations.

04

Responsible Use

Explain that estimates are not guaranteed market prices or purchase advice.
